Who I Am

Where Design Meets Code

I'm a front-end developer in the making with a passion for design, interfaces, and creative problem-solving. I'm fascinated by the space where visual ideas become interactive experiences, where creativity meets logic and design becomes something you can actually use.

I’m still learning, experimenting, and figuring out how software works from the inside out. But somewhere between designing an interface and making it come alive with code, I found the kind of work that makes me want to keep building.
